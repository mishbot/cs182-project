We found that it did not work well to select the refusal direction based on ridge-regularized linear regression on refusal score as a function of activation. The predictor we obtained by this method did not attain high precision and recall as a detector of harmful inputs in comparison to the other two predictors.

However, both logistic regression and difference-of-means-based selection of refusal direction can attain over 99 percent precision and recall as harmful input detectors.
